WOULD OR WOULDN'T

Spicy Couples Drinking Game 🔥

How a Turn Works

You get a prompt
You answer:
❌ "Wouldn't"
You take 1 drink. Next prompt.
✋ "Would"
Your partner can drink 1 or challenge you: "Ok, will you?"
🔥 WILL
You commit. You earn a Token. Partner takes 2 drinks. You have to do it within the week.
🐔 Chicken
You back down. You take 2 drinks. Partner keeps their Token.
🎯 Each partner starts with 2 Tokens.
🏆 First to 4 Tokens wins.
Made for fun • Drink responsibly • Play safe
